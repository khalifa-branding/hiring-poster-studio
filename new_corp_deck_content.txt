After reviewing the deck, my recommendation is not to send the existing 52-slide presentation to prospective clients. It reads more like a comprehensive company profile than a business development deck. For new business, decision-makers typically spend 3–5 minutes on the first read, so the deck should tell a concise story with proof points instead of showcasing every event and capability.
The strongest version would be 12–15 slides that retain only the highest-value information from the current deck while eliminating repetition. All the content below is derived from your attached corporate profile.
